About Us

Welcome to Brushing It Off, the painting club for anyone who's had a long day, a rough week, or just needs an excuse to make a mess on purpose. No experience is necessary, and talent is completely optional.

Three Reasons to Join

Stress Relief one Stroke at a Time

Painting is cheaper than therapy and way more colorful! Trade your worries for a canvas and some paint.

No Talent Needed, Seriously

Stick figures are just as valid as masterpieces! We celebrate effort, not perfection.

New Friends, Good Vibes

Meet new friends, share some laughs, and connect with a community that’s here for the fun and maybe a little gossip between brushstrokes.
